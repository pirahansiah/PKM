This site is a personal knowledge base and engineering notebook maintained by Dr. Farshid Pirahansiah. It collects technical deep dives, course notes, publications, and practical guides on computer vision, deep learning, edge AI, and LLM engineering.

## How the knowledge base is organized

- **Docs** — technical notes on computer vision, CUDA/GPU programming, LLMs, optimization, and developer tools.
- **Courses** — structured notes from courses and workshops on ML, MLOps, ROS, embedded AI, and more.
- **Publications** — papers, patents, journals, books, and keynotes authored or co-authored by the site owner.
- **PKM** — personal knowledge management pages, including an about page and curated link collections.

## How to find things

- Use the **Search** page (magnifier icon in the navigation) to find notes by keyword.
- Use the **Graph** page to explore how notes are linked.
- Use the **WebGPU LLM** page to ask questions about the content of this site in your browser.

## Contributing

This knowledge base is a living document. Content is added and refined continuously, and corrections are welcome via the contact link in the footer.
